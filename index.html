Chapter Five

How We Started

Like every normal people we started talking, but it didn't really start till I asked her — "ARE YOU BEAUTIFUL!!!" In her mind she was like, who is this guy with this audacity (I know say she don de feel me sha 😂😂). After a little back and forth of her screaming and showing me how beautiful she was (she was so beautiful 😩).

After about a month of texting we finally met — sorry guys, we were in two different states when we started talking. I was in Kaduna in uni and she was in Abuja in uni too, but we both lived in Abuja. Back to the story — we met, then I took her on two dates, then asked her out on the third date. I was so shocked she agreed, because I had a smoking problem at the time and I didn't try to hide it 😭😭.

Chapter Six

How I Asked Her Out

It wasn't anything serious or dramatic honestly, it was a bit embarrassing 😭. My exact words were: "Ehhen, what about that your boyfriend sef?" (I don't even know why I said it because I already knew she didn't have one 😂).

She said she didn't, and then I asked if I could be her man… and she said YES. She was blushing so much, I could tell she already felt something for me too 🤭. I was honestly scared to ask because I didn't want to get rejected. I really liked her, and if she had said no, I don't even know what I would've done 😭.

Later she even told her friend who gave me her contact that I was "behaving like a saint" (me? saint? 😭). But in that moment, I just knew the feeling was mutual.
